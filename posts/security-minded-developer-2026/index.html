Who we are

About OpenCRVS

Since 2018, we have been building OpenCRVS: open-source civil registration and vital statistics (CRVS) software that helps governments record births, deaths and other life events. OpenCRVS is a not-for-profit initiative and is recognised as a Digital Public Good.

Without a birth certificate, a child does not exist in the eyes of the law. Around 1 in 4 children under five worldwide are still unregistered, and over 100 countries lack a fully functioning CRVS system. OpenCRVS is designed to change that. It works on mobile, in low-connectivity settings, and is built around the people who use it every day.

We currently support projects in the Philippines, Burkina Faso, Uganda, Somalia and the Pacific Islands. Learn more about our UN Development Partnership, our technical documentation and our code on GitHub.

Why it matters

The role

OpenCRVS holds some of the most sensitive personal data that a government keeps: the births, deaths, parents and identities of its citizens. Governments host OpenCRVS themselves, often in their own data centres, and they need to trust that the software keeps this data safe. That trust starts with the code we write. That is where you come in.

As a Security-minded Software Developer, you will build OpenCRVS features with our core team, and you will be the developer who makes sure they are secure. You will write well-tested TypeScript across the stack, review pull requests with a security eye, own security findings from triage to release, and help the rest of the team make security part of every stage of development. You will work alongside our CTO, Technical Architect, DevOps team and core developers.

You will join a team of around 10 technical leads & senior developers, part of a 30 strong team of designers, QA testers, business analysts and managers, based across Europe (Paris, London, Helsinki, Kiev and Lisbon). We will invest in getting you up to speed on OpenCRVS through onboarding and pairing with the core team.

Responsibilities

What you will do

Build the product

  • Develop new OpenCRVS Core features across the stack, in TypeScript, Node.js and React, alongside the core team.
  • Write well-tested code, and flag technical debt and architectural risks early.
  • Work with our Product Owner to turn requirements into clear, secure designs.

Build security in

  • Design and build authentication, authorisation, audit logging and the handling of personal data.
  • Threat model new features and integrations before they are built, and turn the findings into tickets.
  • Add security testing and dependency scanning to our CI pipelines with GitHub Actions.

Own security findings

  • Triage and prioritise security findings and vulnerability reports from the countries we work with, penetration testers, automated scanners and dependency alerts.
  • Fix the issues yourself and take each one through our whole development process, from ticket to code review, testing and release, with support from other developers.
  • Review pull requests and designs for security issues, and prepare hotfix releases and GitHub Security Advisories for supported OpenCRVS versions.

Support safe deployments

  • Work with our DevOps team on hardening Kubernetes, networks and secrets in government deployments.
  • Help answer security questions from government clients, auditors and penetration testers.
  • Keep our security documentation and threat models up to date as the product changes.

A typical week

  • Daily stand-up with the whole team
  • Feature development, bug fixing and pair programming
  • Code reviews, with a focus on security, authentication and personal data
  • Triaging and fixing dependency alerts, scanner results and vulnerability reports
  • Threat modelling and design discussions for new features and integrations
  • Working with our DevOps team on secure deployments
  • Occasional international travel to partner countries, typically up to 2 trips per year, one to two weeks each.
