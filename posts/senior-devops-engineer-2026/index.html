Who we are

About OpenCRVS

Since 2018, we have been building OpenCRVS: free, open-source software for civil registration and vital statistics (CRVS) that helps governments register births, deaths and other life events. OpenCRVS is a not-for-profit initiative. It is a digital public good, recognised by the Digital Public Goods Alliance.

Without a birth certificate, a child does not exist in the eyes of the law. Around 1 in 4 children under five worldwide are still unregistered, and over 100 countries lack a fully functioning CRVS system. OpenCRVS is built to change that. It works offline and on modest hardware, and it is designed around the people who use it every day.

Sources: UNICEF, Birth Registration for Every Child by 2030: Are We on Track?, 2019 (1 in 4 children under five) · World Bank and WHO, Global Civil Registration and Vital Statistics Scaling Up Investment Plan 2015–2024, 2014 (over 100 countries) · DPG Registry, digitalpublicgoods.net/r/opencrvs

We currently support projects in the Philippines, Burkina Faso, Uganda, Somalia and the Pacific Islands. Learn more about our UN Development Partnership, our technical documentation and our code on GitHub.

Why it matters

The role

OpenCRVS is critical government infrastructure. Governments host it themselves, often in their own data centres, so it has to be installed, secured, monitored and maintained in close partnership with ministries, their support teams and their developers. That is where you come in.

As our Senior DevOps Engineer, you will own the infrastructure side of OpenCRVS deployments: servers, networking, automation, security and monitoring. You will work alongside our DevOps Lead, Technical Architect and the core team, and help country teams run their installations safely and confidently.

You will join a team of around 10 technical leads and senior developers, part of a 30-strong team of designers, QA testers, business analysts and managers, based across Europe (Paris, London, Helsinki, Kyiv and Lisbon). We will invest in getting you up to speed on OpenCRVS through onboarding and pairing with the core team.

Responsibilities

What you will do

Deploy and configure

  • Install and configure OpenCRVS infrastructure to meet each country's civil registration requirements, from pilot to national rollout.
  • Provision servers and hosting environments with automation, and set up networking, VPNs and firewalls.
  • Configure application health monitoring, logging and alerting for every installation.

Keep live systems healthy and secure

  • Monitor and maintain the health, security, backups and performance of live OpenCRVS instances.
  • Debug issues in government infrastructure, virtualisation and VPNs, almost always remotely.
  • Lead level 3 investigation and root cause analysis, and run quality assurance reviews of in-country deployments against our infrastructure recommendations.

Automate and improve

  • Develop and review OpenCRVS core DevOps and automation features.
  • Work with our Product Owner to turn field learning into clear tickets for DevOps, automation and testing.
  • Contribute to the OpenCRVS documentation where needed.

Advise and support

  • Advise our CTO and Technical Architect on production deployment architectures that maximise security, performance and resilience.
  • Be a trusted technical contact for governments and their support teams.
  • Guide country teams through applying hotfixes and upgrades safely.

A typical week

  • Daily stand-up with the whole team
  • DevOps development, automation, bug fixing and pair programming
  • Working with our DevOps Lead and with government support and development teams
  • Code reviews and architecture discussions for new features and integrations
  • Estimating upcoming work and flagging technical debt
  • Configuring, deploying and testing OpenCRVS installations
  • Occasional international travel to partner countries, typically up to 2 trips per year, one to two weeks each
